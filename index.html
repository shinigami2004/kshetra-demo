THE IDEA

From recommendation
to a recorded outcome.

A farmer should be able to understand why work is proposed, stay in control of the decision, and see evidence after the task. Kshetra connects these moments.

01 / SENSE

Read the field

Bring available soil, pump, weather and farm records into a plot-level view.

02 / DECIDE

Suggest the next task

AI proposes timing and flags exceptions. Safety rules and the owner decide what can happen.

03 / VERIFY

Close the loop

Track execution, proof, failures and cost in an action ledger the owner can review.

WHY THIS APPROACH

Control belongs
with the farmer.

Kshetra starts with recommendations and owner-approved work. An action is useful only when the owner can understand it, stop it and see what took place in the field.

Explore the decision flow ↗
01

Approval before execution

Plot rules and owner permission stand between a recommendation and an external action.

02

Exceptions stay visible

Power loss, missing proof or an incomplete task should surface for review, not disappear into a success message.

03

Pay for verified work

The proposed per-action fee applies to work that meets an agreed proof rule. A spending cap can keep the seasonal bill predictable.
